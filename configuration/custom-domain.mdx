By default, your Mintlify site is accessible at a subdomain under `mintlify.app`. You can point any custom domain or subdomain to your docs in just a few steps — no server configuration required. Once verified, Mintlify automatically provisions and renews your SSL certificate.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- Access to the DNS settings for your domain (through your domain registrar or DNS provider)
- A Mintlify account on the **Pro plan or higher**

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Custom Domain settings">
    Log in to the [Mintlify dashboard](https://dashboard.mintlify.com) and navigate to **Settings → Custom Domain**. This page is where you manage all domain configuration for your site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your desired domain">
    Type the domain or subdomain you want to use — for example, `docs.yourcompany.com`. Most teams use a subdomain like `docs.` to keep their main domain separate from the documentation site. Click **Save** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the CNAME record value">
    After saving, Mintlify displays a CNAME record value. Copy this value — you'll need it in the next step. It looks something like `hosting.mintlify.app`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a CNAME record at your DNS provider">
    Log in to your domain registrar or DNS provider and create a new DNS record with the following values:

    | Field | Value |
    |-------|-------|
    | **Type** | `CNAME` |
    | **Name / Host** | `docs` (or whichever subdomain you chose) |
    | **Value / Target** | The value you copied from the Mintlify dashboard |
    | **TTL** | `3600` (or your provider's default) |

    Save the record. DNS changes can take anywhere from a few minutes to 48 hours to propagate globally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify ownership in the dashboard">
    Return to **Settings → Custom Domain** in the Mintlify dashboard and click **Verify**. Mintlify checks for your CNAME record and confirms ownership. If DNS hasn't fully propagated yet, wait a few minutes and try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="SSL certificate provisioned automatically">
    Once your domain is verified, Mintlify automatically provisions a free SSL certificate for your domain. Your docs site will be available over HTTPS — no additional configuration required on your end.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Mintlify handles HTTPS entirely — you don't need to upload certificates, configure TLS, or manage renewals. SSL provisioning typically completes within a few minutes of successful domain verification.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

If something isn't working as expected after following the steps above, the issues below cover the most common causes.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="CNAME not verifying">
    DNS propagation can take up to 48 hours depending on your provider and the TTL value set on your record. Before contacting support, use a public DNS checker (such as [dnschecker.org](https://dnschecker.org)) to confirm that your CNAME record is visible from multiple locations around the world.

    Double-check that:
    - The **Name/Host** field is set to only the subdomain portion (e.g., `docs`) — not the full domain (e.g., `docs.yourcompany.com`)
    - The **Value/Target** exactly matches the string Mintlify provided — no extra spaces or characters
    - You haven't accidentally created a conflicting `A` record for the same subdomain
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Site still shows the mintlify.app domain">
    If your CNAME has been verified but your browser still shows the old `mintlify.app` URL, try the following:

    - **Clear your browser cache** — cached DNS results can persist even after the records have updated
    - **Wait for TTL expiry** — if your old record had a long TTL, resolvers may continue using the cached value until it expires
    - **Try an incognito/private window** — this bypasses most local caching and gives you a clean lookup
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="SSL certificate not provisioning">
    SSL provisioning begins automatically as soon as domain verification succeeds. In most cases, the certificate appears within a few minutes. If your domain shows as verified in the dashboard but HTTPS is still not working after **1 hour**, reach out to [Mintlify support](https://mintlify.com/support) with your domain name and account email — the team can manually trigger provisioning or investigate any errors.

    <Warning>
      Do not attempt to manually upload a certificate through your DNS provider or CDN for the same subdomain. Conflicts between Mintlify's provisioned certificate and an externally managed one can prevent HTTPS from working correctly.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
