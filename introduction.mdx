Mintlify is a documentation platform designed for developer teams who want beautiful, maintainable docs without the overhead of building and hosting a site from scratch. You write content in MDX, store it in your Git repository, and Mintlify handles everything else — builds, deploys, hosting, search, and versioning. The result is a fast, polished docs site that stays in sync with your codebase.

## How it works

Mintlify follows a straightforward Git-based workflow. You write content locally, push it to GitHub, and Mintlify takes care of the rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write in MDX">
    Create and edit `.mdx` files in your docs folder. MDX gives you the full power of Markdown plus the ability to embed interactive React-based components anywhere in your content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Push to Git">
    Commit your changes and push them to the branch connected to your Mintlify project. You can also open a pull request — Mintlify generates a preview deployment for every PR so you can review changes before they go live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mintlify deploys automatically">
    Mintlify detects the push, builds your site, and publishes the updated pages within seconds. No manual deployment steps, no build scripts to maintain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's in your docs repo

Mintlify needs only two things in your repository to build your site:

**`docs.json`** is the central configuration file for your entire docs site. It controls your site name, logo, brand colors, navigation structure, custom domain, and dozens of other settings. Think of it as the single source of truth for how your docs look and behave. You'll find yourself editing it whenever you add new pages to the navigation, update your branding, or enable new platform features.

**MDX files** are your content pages. Each `.mdx` file becomes a page on your site. Your folder structure directly maps to your URL structure — a file at `guides/authentication.mdx` is served at `/guides/authentication`. You can organize pages into as many nested folders as your content requires. Any MDX file not referenced in the navigation section of `docs.json` will still be accessible by direct URL, but it won't appear in the sidebar.
