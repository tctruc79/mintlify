## Get up and running in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.mintlify.com](https://app.mintlify.com). No credit card required to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your repository">
    Link your GitHub or GitLab repository from the Mintlify dashboard. Mintlify watches your default branch for changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure docs.json">
    Open `docs.json` at the root of your docs folder. Set your site name, colors, logo, and navigation structure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Push to deploy">
    Commit your MDX pages and push to your connected branch. Mintlify builds and publishes your site automatically — no CI pipeline required.
  </Step>
</Steps>
