Mintlify is the documentation platform built for modern developer teams. Connect your Git repository, write pages in MDX, and publish a beautiful, fully hosted docs site — no infrastructure to manage, no CI pipeline to configure. Every push to your repo is a deploy.

## Get up and running in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.mintlify.com](https://app.mintlify.com). No credit card required to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your repository">
    Link your GitHub repository from the Mintlify dashboard. Mintlify watches your default branch and triggers a new build on every push.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure docs.json">
    Open `docs.json` at the root of your docs folder. Set your site name, colors, logo, and navigation structure to match your brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Push to deploy">
    Commit your MDX pages and push to your connected branch. Mintlify builds and publishes your site automatically within seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>
